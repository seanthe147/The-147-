# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public venue discovery and conversion pages served to anonymous visitors
- Public membership pages and legal/compliance pages
- Public marketing preview pages under `/test-site`
- The Expo web app's public customer-facing routes when exported for web

## Out of scope
- Authenticated customer account areas
- Staff, admin, HR, kiosk, and internal operations surfaces
- Native iOS/Android app store SEO outside the web source code

## Target audience
- People in and around Bradford looking for snooker, pool, dining, events, memberships, or venue hire
- Existing customers trying to book tables, view memberships, or contact the venue
