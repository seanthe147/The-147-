The 147 — Cookie & Storage Policy

Snooker · Bar · Restaurant

📱 Device Storage Only — No Tracking Cookies

Last Updated: March 2026

No Web Cookies

The 147 app is a native mobile application. We do not use web browser cookies or any third-party tracking cookies. There are no advertising trackers, no cross-site tracking, and no analytics cookies.

What We Store on Your Device

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device using AsyncStorage (React Native's local storage). This data stays on your device and is never sold or shared with third parties.

🔒
Consent Preferences
Your privacy consent choices (analytics, marketing) and the date you gave consent. Stored so you are not asked every time you open the app.
🔑
Session Token
If you sign in to your account, an authentication token is stored securely so you stay logged in between sessions. Cleared when you sign out.
🏆
Loyalty Points Cache
Your current loyalty points balance is cached locally to display instantly without a network request. Refreshed automatically when you open the app.

What We Do Not Store

We do not store payment card details, passwords, or any sensitive personal data in local storage. Booking and account data is held on our secure servers only.

Third-Party Content

Some screens embed third-party services (OrderTab for food ordering, TicketSource for event tickets). These load in an in-app browser and may use their own cookies subject to their own privacy policies.

Clearing Local Data

To clear locally stored consent preferences in the app, go to About → Privacy Policy → Reset Privacy Preferences. To clear your session, sign out from My Account.

See also our Privacy Policy and UK GDPR Rights.
