Good to Know

The small print.

Where can it be spent?

Anywhere in the venue — table hire, food, drinks, ticketed events, even towards a membership subscription.

How long are they valid?

12 months from date of purchase. The recipient gets a clear expiry reminder via email a month before.

Can I split the value?

Yes — gift cards work like cash. Spend across multiple visits, on different things, with no minimum spend per visit.

What if I lose it?

Each card has a unique code linked to the recipient's email. Lost it? We can resend the digital card on request.
