Kiosk Orders — Staff Cheat Sheet

How kiosk orders work and what staff need to do. Pin this near the till.

How the kiosk works (the short version)

  • Card-only. The kiosk takes card payments at the counter terminal — no cash, no "pay at counter later".
  • Customer adds items on the kiosk, types their name & table number, and taps Pay by card.
  • The counter terminal lights up. Customer taps, inserts or swipes their card on the terminal.
  • Once payment goes through, the order fires straight to the bar/kitchen KDS. Staff don't need to do anything on the till.
  • Customer sees their order number on the kiosk screen and waits at their table for delivery.
Normal day, you do nothing. Kiosk orders pay themselves on the terminal and appear on the KDS automatically. The till just records them like any other card sale.

If a customer says "the card terminal won't work"

  1. Check the terminal is on, has signal, and isn't mid-transaction with another customer.
  2. Ask the customer to try a different card if possible.
  3. If it still won't go through, the kiosk will show "Card payment unavailable, please order at the counter". The kiosk order is automatically cancelled — nothing is owed and nothing fires to the KDS.
  4. Take their order on the till as you would any other walk-up customer (cash or card on the till).
Important: If for any reason a kiosk order does appear in the till's Online Orders / Pickup queue (rare — only happens if the terminal pushed but the customer never tapped a card, or if a previous version of the app left an unpaid order behind), do NOT charge it on the till. Doing so closes the order without firing the food/drink to the KDS. Instead, find the order on the staff dashboard (/staff → Orders & Menu → Orders), take the payment physically, and tap the green Mark Paid button. That fires the prep ticket to the KDS.

If a customer says "I paid but my food/drink hasn't come"

  1. Open the staff dashboard at /staff → Orders & Menu → Orders.
  2. Find the order by name or kiosk ticket number (the blue KIOSK #42 badge).
  3. Check its status:
    • Paid — the KDS has the order. Check the bar/kitchen for the holdup.
    • Pay at counter — the terminal payment didn't actually complete. Take payment now and tap Mark Paid.
    • Cancelled — the order was voided. The customer wasn't charged. Re-take the order on the till.

If a customer wants to add or cancel items after ordering

  • Add items: their kiosk order is already paid, so just take the new items as a fresh sale on the till.
  • Cancel: open the order on the staff dashboard and tap Cancel order. If they were already charged, refund through the Square dashboard separately.
