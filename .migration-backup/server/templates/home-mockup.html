Why The 147

A proper club, not a games arcade.

We built The 147 for players who care about the cloth. Tournament-grade tables, knowledgeable staff, and an atmosphere that suits a quiet practice session or a lively league night equally well.

Tournament-Spec Tables

Professionally-maintained snooker, American pool and English 8-ball tables — re-clothed regularly, levelled to spec, and ready for serious play.

Bar & Kitchen

Full licensed bar with craft beers and signature cocktails. Kitchen serving stone-baked pizzas, sharers and small plates until late.

Memberships from £7.99

Three flexible tiers with discounted table rates, guest passes, loyalty rewards and members-only tournament entry.
