Staff Privacy Notice

This notice explains what personal data The 147 Bradford ("we", "us", "our") collects about staff members, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and your rights under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Last updated: April 2026

1. Who Is Responsible for Your Data?

The 147 Bradford is the data controller for all staff personal data. If you have any questions about how your data is used, please speak to your manager or contact us using the details at the bottom of this notice.

3. How We Protect Your Data

  • Your PIN is never stored — only a one-way cryptographic hash is kept.
  • GPS coordinates are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before being written to the database.
  • Leave request reasons and incident descriptions are encrypted at rest.
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher).
  • Access to HR records is restricted to managers and owners only.
  • Server logs are redacted to prevent PII appearing in log files.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell your data. We only share it where necessary:

  • Expo / Apple / Google — to deliver push notifications to your device. Only your device token is shared; no personal details.
  • Hosting provider — Replit provides the server infrastructure. Your data is stored in their database as encrypted values.

6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Right of AccessRequest a copy of all data held about you (Subject Access Request). We must respond within 30 days.
Right to RectificationAsk us to correct inaccurate data (e.g. wrong clock-in time). Speak to your manager.
Right to ErasureAsk us to delete your data, subject to legal retention obligations (e.g. we must keep payroll records for 7 years).
Right to Restrict ProcessingAsk us to pause processing your data while a dispute is resolved.
Right to ObjectObject to processing based on legitimate interests. You cannot object to processing based on legal obligation.
Right to Withdraw ConsentWithdraw GPS consent at any time in the HR app settings. This will not affect data already recorded.

7. Withdrawing GPS Consent

You can withdraw consent for GPS location collection at any time. To do this, go to the HR app and select Settings → Revoke GPS Consent, or speak to your manager who can remove consent on your behalf. Withdrawing consent means future clock-in/out events will not record your location; data already recorded will remain until the 3-year GPS retention period expires.

8. How to Exercise Your Rights

To make a Subject Access Request or exercise any of your rights above, speak to a manager or contact us directly. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe your data has been mishandled:

9. Changes to This Notice

We will update this notice if our data processing practices change. Significant changes will be communicated to you directly via the staff app. The current version is always available at /staff-privacy-notice.
