Kiosk Orders — Staff Cheat Sheet

How to find, charge, and clear orders placed at the customer kiosk. Pin this near the till.

Where to find a kiosk order on the till

  1. On the Square for Restaurants till, tap the bag-shaped icon on the left side menu — this is the Online Orders / Pickup queue.
  2. Kiosk orders show with the customer's name and table number, e.g. "Sean · Table 235", with the amount on the right.
  3. Tap the order to open it.
Don't look in the dine-in Open Tickets tab. Kiosk orders never appear there — only in the Pickup queue.

Taking cash payment

  1. Open the order from the Pickup queue (steps above).
  2. Tap Charge (or Review and Pay, depending on till version).
  3. Tap Cash.
  4. Type in the amount the customer handed you.
  5. Tap Confirm — the till will tell you how much change to give back.
  6. Choose Print / Email / No receipt per the customer.
Once charged, the order closes automatically and fires to the bar/kitchen KDS. You don't need to do anything else.

Taking card payment

  1. Open the order from the Pickup queue.
  2. Tap Charge.
  3. Insert, tap, or swipe the card on the reader as you would for any other order.
  4. Receipt prompt — same as cash.

Common situations

"I already paid at the kiosk" — if the order is still in the Pickup queue, the kiosk payment didn't complete. Take payment again at the till. Square only charges once per tender, so there's no risk of double-charging.
Customer wants to add or remove items — open the order, tap Edit at the top, make the changes, then charge as normal. Square recalculates the total automatically.
Customer wants to cancel — open the order, tap the ··· menu, choose Void. Confirm the reason. The order closes without payment.
I can't find the order — ask the customer for their ticket number, then check the staff web dashboard at /staff. Every kiosk order shows there too with a "PAY AT COUNTER" badge. If it's there but not in the till's Pickup queue, the till may need a force-quit and reopen.
