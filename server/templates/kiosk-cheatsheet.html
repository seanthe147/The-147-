Kiosk Orders — Staff Cheat Sheet

How to take counter payment for orders placed at the customer kiosk. Pin this near the till.

Important: Do NOT charge kiosk orders on the Square till. Doing so closes the order without sending it to the bar/kitchen KDS, and the drink/food will never get made. Always use the staff dashboard "Mark Paid" button (steps below).

Step-by-step: taking payment for a kiosk order

  1. Customer arrives at the counter with a kiosk ticket number, e.g. "Order #42", and offers cash or card.
  2. On the staff dashboard (/staff), open the Orders & Menu → Orders tab. Find the order with the matching ticket number — it'll have a blue KIOSK #42 badge and a PAY AT COUNTER tag.
  3. Take the payment physically:
    • Cash — count the cash, give change from the till drawer.
    • Card — charge it on the card reader as a manual sale (no need to attach to a Square order).
  4. On the dashboard, tap the green Mark Paid button on that order's row.
  5. The order automatically fires to the bar/kitchen KDS. The customer is told their order is being prepared.
That's it. Mark Paid does two things at once: it records the order as paid in our books, and tells Square to fire the prep ticket to the KDS. No till tap needed.

Why we don't use the till to charge kiosk orders

Square treats a kiosk order as an "Online Order / Pickup" ticket. If staff press Charge on the till for one of these, Square assumes it's a walk-in sale and instantly closes the order — without firing it to the KDS. The bar/kitchen never sees the order, and the customer waits forever.

The Mark Paid button on the staff dashboard goes through a different Square flow that keeps the order open until the KDS fires the prep ticket, then closes it cleanly. Both cash and card-at-counter use this same path.

Common situations

"I already paid at the kiosk" — if the order shows PAID on the dashboard already, no action needed. Just tell the customer their order is being prepared. If it still shows PAY AT COUNTER, the kiosk payment didn't complete — take the payment now.
Customer wants to add or remove items after ordering — easiest is to mark the existing order paid first (so the original items fire to KDS), then start a fresh sale on the till for the extra items.
Customer wants to cancel before paying — on the dashboard, open the order and tap Cancel order. The kiosk ticket is voided and nothing fires to KDS.
Order isn't showing on the dashboard — tap the Refresh button on the Orders tab. If it's still missing, the kiosk lost its connection — ask the customer to retry their order.
