The 147 — Privacy Policy

Snooker · Bar · Restaurant

🔒 UK GDPR Compliant

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Who We Are

The 147 ("we", "us", "our") is the data controller for the personal data processed through this mobile application. We are a snooker venue, bar, and restaurant operating in the United Kingdom.

For any data protection queries, please contact us via our website: www.the147.co.uk

2. What Data We Collect

This app is designed with privacy in mind. We minimise the data we collect to only what is necessary:

  • Device preferences: Your consent choices are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Table bookings: When you book a table, we collect your name, email address, and phone number. This data is stored securely on our servers and is necessary to manage your reservation. Booking data is automatically anonymised after 12 months.
  • Push notifications: If you opt in, your device token is stored to send you venue updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Contact form messages: When you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, optional phone number, subject, and message. Contact form data is retained for 12 months then automatically deleted.
  • Customer accounts: If you create an account, we store your name, email address, phone number (optional), and a securely hashed password. Your password is never stored in plain text.
  • Food and drink ordering: Ordering is provided through OrderTab (ordertab.menu) embedded within the app. Their privacy policy applies to those transactions.
  • Event tickets: Ticket purchases are handled by TicketSource (ticketsource.com). Their privacy policy applies to those transactions.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR (the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018), we process data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract (Article 6(1)(b)): Processing booking data and customer account data is necessary to fulfil your reservation and manage your account.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): For contact form submissions, push notifications, and optional analytics or marketing. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Operating the app and providing venue information, offers, and event listings.

4. Third-Party Services

This app integrates with the following third-party services. When you use these services, their respective privacy policies apply:

  • The 147 Website (the147.co.uk) — for online table bookings
  • OrderTab (ordertab.menu) — for food and drink ordering
  • TicketSource (ticketsource.com) — for event ticket purchases

5. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Under the UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Article 15): Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification (Article 16): Ask us to correct any inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure (Article 17): Ask us to delete your personal data.
  • Right to restrict processing (Article 18): Ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability (Article 20): Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object (Article 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at www.the147.co.uk.

6. Data Retention

  • Consent preferences: Stored locally on your device until you reset them or uninstall the app.
  • Booking data: Retained for 12 months after the booking date, then automatically anonymised.
  • Contact form messages: Retained for 12 months after submission, then automatically deleted.
  • Customer account data: Retained until you delete your account. Deletion removes all associated data permanently.
  • Push notification tokens: Retained until you unsubscribe or uninstall the app.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data (Article 32 of the UK GDPR), including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data transfers
  • Field-level encryption (AES-256-GCM) for booking personal data
  • Secure password hashing (scrypt) — your password is never stored in readable form
  • Local-only storage of app preferences and consent choices

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your personal data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

  • Website: ico.org.uk
  • Telephone: 0303 123 1113

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
