The Kitchen

Properly good food, all day.

Our kitchen team works with locally-sourced produce wherever possible. The menu's built around what plays well with a pint and a frame — stone-baked pizzas, sharing boards, classic mains and a few seasonal specials.

Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options available throughout. All allergens documented — just ask any member of staff.
