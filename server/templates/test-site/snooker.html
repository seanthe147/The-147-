The Tables

Built for serious play.

Every table at The 147 is professionally maintained — re-clothed regularly, levelled, and serviced by experienced engineers. Whether you're practising for a county fixture or having a casual frame after work, you'll find the conditions you'd expect from a proper venue.

Walk-ins are welcome and tables are available on a first-come basis. To guarantee your slot — particularly evenings and weekends — book ahead online.

Leagues & Tournaments

Play competitively.

We run handicap leagues most evenings and host monthly ranking tournaments. Open to all — members get reduced entry fees and priority booking.

Monday Snooker League

Handicap-based, drawn pairs, 14-week season. £4 weekly entry. Trophy & cash prizes for top 3.

Wednesday Pool League

American pool 9-ball doubles. Open to teams of 2. £6 per pair, weekly cash pot.

Monthly Open Tournaments

Ranking tournaments first Saturday of every month. £15 entry, prizes for top 4.
